Connections Summary:
  Pico GP2  → eInk SCK      (SPI0 clock)
  Pico GP3  → eInk MOSI     (SPI0 data out)
  Pico GP4  → eInk MISO     (SPI0 data in, for SD card)
  Pico GP5  → eInk ECS      (E-Ink chip select)
  Pico GP6  → eInk D/C      (Data/Command)
  Pico GP7  → eInk RST      (Reset)
  Pico GP8  → eInk BUSY     (Busy flag)
  Pico GP9  → eInk SDCS     (SD card chip select)
  Pico 3V3  → eInk VIN      (Power)
  Pico GND  → eInk GND      (Ground)
  Pico GP10 → BTN_UP        (Active low, internal pull-up)
  Pico GP11 → BTN_DOWN      (Active low, internal pull-up)
  Pico GP12 → BTN_SEL       (Active low, internal pull-up)
  All buttons → GND
